A few words about us

Advocates on your side of the table

RayNayel is an independent property advocacy practice helping Australians buy, sell and invest with the same information and discipline the professionals use.

Book a discovery callExplore our services

Property advocate · CEO of RayNayel

Tariq Butt

“I am a property advocate. I help people in buying and selling their properties by working on the best price, free legal advice and all forms of documentation.”

Tariq founded RayNayel on a simple observation: in almost every property transaction, the professional negotiators are on the other side of the table. Selling agents transact every week; most buyers and sellers transact only a handful of times in a lifetime. That imbalance costs everyday Australians money.

From building your search criteria and screening on and off-market listings, to due diligence, negotiation, auction bidding and settlement, we act as your representative at every step.

Why we exist

Buying and selling property shouldn’t feel like a gamble

A home is usually the largest financial decision of your life, made under time pressure, in a market that moves weekly, against professionals who negotiate daily. We built RayNayel so no client has to face that alone.

Today we support home buyers, sellers and investors across Melbourne and beyond with unbiased opinions, valuable local knowledge and one measure of success: the best possible outcome for you.
