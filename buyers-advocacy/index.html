The case for representation

Why hire a buyer’s advocate?

It is your best chance to find the right property at the lowest defensible price, with expert advice at every step.

We save you time and hassle, highlight issues that may affect the transaction, assist investors with rental appraisals, guide market timing and protect your interests in a process where everyone else is paid by the seller.

  • Auction and full-process support

  • Independent view of true market value

Understanding the service

What is buyer advocacy?

A buyer’s advocate works for the buyer—finding suitable properties, negotiating a fair price and moving quickly when the right opportunity appears.

We work with you face to face and behind the scenes, informing you about every relevant aspect of the property before you sign. The result is a buying experience with fewer surprises, better information and your interests at the centre.

We make it easy for you

Buying is exciting and, at times, frustrating. We guide the process, answer questions early and make sure emotion never replaces evidence.
